01 — Our StorySince 10 January 2018

It Started with Two Families and a Promise

Sometimes God starts something much bigger than we realise—with something very small.

For us, it started with two families, an open home, and a simple decision: we would show up every Wednesday and pursue what God had placed on our hearts.

2018

On 10 January 2018, we gathered in our home in Auckland to pray.

There was no big plan. No crowd. No impressive programme. Just a hunger for God.

We believed God was calling us to something deeper—to know Jesus, to grow in His Word, to learn to recognise His presence, and to become people who didn't just talk about faith, but actually lived it.

So we kept showing up. Week after week. Some Wednesdays, there were only a few of us. Other Wednesdays, the house was full. There were seasons when it would have been easy to wonder, “Is this really going anywhere?”

  • ButWe kept praying.
  • AndWe kept opening the Bible.
  • AndWe kept encouraging one another.
  • AndWe kept making room for the Holy Spirit.

And slowly, something began to happen. People's lives began to change.

Families were strengthened. People found Jesus. Some were baptised. Others experienced healing, deliverance, restoration and a renewed hunger for God.

We began to see that what started as a simple Wednesday gathering was becoming something much bigger than a weekly meeting.

It was becoming a community of disciples. And that is where Emmanuel is today.

02 — Where It BeganAn open home

It Began with an Open Door

“Behold, the former things have come to pass, and new things I declare; before they spring forth I tell you of them.”

The promise · Isaiah 42:8–9

Rohan & Shilpa Emmanuel · with their daughter Anaiah

Rohan and Shilpa Emmanuel have been married since 2013, and the journey that led them to a shared calling sits at the heart of this story. On 10 January 2018, holding on to a promise God had given them, they opened their Auckland home for a simple Wednesday prayer.

Together, they share a passion for teaching the Word in a way that is practical, relevant and life-changing. At home, their daughter Anaiah fills the house with energy and joy, whether she is tumbling through her gymnastics routines, playing her guitar or simply enjoying time with family.

03 — The Journey2018 — Now

Year by Year, Wednesday by Wednesday

  1. 2018

    Two families. One promise.

    On 10 January 2018, a simple Wednesday prayer began in our home in Auckland. We had no idea what God was about to do.

  2. 2019

    Room for more.

    The circle outgrew the two families and the one living room. Friends and neighbours arrived with their guitars, their questions, and their prayers, and every Wednesday the house filled with worship.

  3. 2020

    Held together.

    When the world locked down, we didn't stop. We moved online, and people from across the world joined us, hungry for prayer and a place to belong in uncertain times.

  4. 2021

    Hunger for His presence.

    God stirred in us a desire to seek His face with no agenda and no plan, so we began all-night prayer. People received healing, deliverance, and the gifts of the Spirit.

  5. 2022

    Leaders raised.

    Brothers and sisters began asking to start meetings in their own homes. We launched a discipleship programme and equipped them with the tools to lead and the accountability to stay faithful.

  6. 2023

    Reaching the broken.

    We grew stronger in our commitment to outreach and evangelism. We found joy in reaching the broken, and a young generation was trained to be sent out.

  7. 2024

    Born again, baptised.

    More people gave their lives to Jesus, and baptisms began. The new believers we had prayed for publicly declared their faith.

  8. 2025

    Seven years of faithfulness.

    We celebrated seven years of God's faithfulness and mercy, remembering how a simple Wednesday prayer had carried us this far.

  9. 2026 Now

    Stronger together.

    We began a structured study of the life of Jesus, with practical, scenario-based application for today's world. We also partnered with Revival House of Prayer and now gather for fasting and prayer on the last Wednesday of every month.

05 — Why We GatherVision · Mission · Purpose

Why We Gather

01 — Our Vision

To be salt and light to our communities, our nation and the world, with Wednesday gatherings in every part of Auckland, and beyond.

02 — Our Mission

To continually become true disciples of Jesus by equipping ourselves and others to practise the presence of God, to see people saved, healed, delivered, transformed, equipped and empowered by the Holy Spirit.

03 — Our Purpose

To spread the love of Jesus to the communities we belong to, and to intercede in prayer with a burden for nations, individuals and families.

Wednesday is the beginningNot the end

We believe Scripture was never given simply to fill our heads with knowledge. It was given to transform the way we live.

So when we gather, we don't want Wednesday to be the end of something. We want it to be the beginning of something that continues when we walk out the door.

  • Into our homes. 01
  • Into our marriages and families. 02
  • Into our workplaces. 03
  • Into our friendships. 04
  • Into our neighbourhoods. 05

Into the places God has called us to serve.

06 — What We DoWednesday Discipleship

More Than a Meeting. A Rhythm of Discipleship.

Three things draw us back to the same living rooms, week after week.

01 / We gather to

Seek His Presence

We pray. We worship. We make room for the Holy Spirit—not as a religious routine, but as a real and living relationship with God.

02 / We gather to

Grow in His Word

We open Scripture together and allow God's Word to teach us, challenge us, correct us and equip us for the life He has called us to live.

03 / We gather to

Stand in the Gap

We pray for individuals, families, communities and nations. Because our burden cannot stop at the walls of our homes.

Our one challenge

And perhaps most importantly, we challenge one another to practise what we learn.

Because it is possible to sit in Bible studies for years and still never allow the Word of God to change the way we live. We don't want that.

We want to be people who hear the Word, believe the Word, and live the Word.

09 — FAQBefore you come

Questions, Gently Answered

Do I need to belong to a church to attend?

No. But if you do have a church, we encourage you to stay faithful to it. Emmanuel is here to strengthen you, not replace your local church.

Is this a church or a denomination?

Neither. We're a discipleship and prayer community made up of believers from many different churches.

What happens on a Wednesday?

We pray, worship, open the Word, practise the presence of God, share testimonies and encouragement, and intercede for people and nations.

Can I bring my family or a friend?

Absolutely. Everyone is welcome.

What if I'm new to all this, or I'm still figuring out my faith?

Come as you are. We welcome people just the way Jesus did.

How do I get the address?

Tap “Join Us” and we'll send you the details for the nearest gathering.

Can I host a Wednesday in my area?

Yes! We'd love to help you start one. Get in touch.
